# AICTE Approval Process Handbook 2024-25 to 2026-27: digest for SIH26261

**Source:** AICTE Approval Process Handbook 2024-25 to 2026-27 (official PDF, 231 pages). Clause numbers below are the handbook's own.

## Process facts (Chapters I–II)
- **2.1(c):** every institution applies **every year** for EoA through the portal.
- **2.1(e):** a lease-based institution gets no EoA when the remaining lease equals the longest course duration, unless the lease is extended by 30 years.
- **2.3.3:**
  - the portal generates a **Deficiency Report**;
  - data locks on final submission;
  - one reopening is allowed (Format 3 + TER + penalty).
- **2.3.4:**
  - (a) all documents are uploaded **digitally signed with DSC** as per Annexure-2;
  - (b) **Affidavit 2** goes on ₹100 non-judicial or e-stamp paper and is digitally signed; false information leads to civil and/or criminal action;
  - (c) scrutiny and EVC are "strictly based on the documents uploaded on AICTE portal".
- **2.3.5:**
  - (a) EVC (inspection) is **random**;
  - (b) a fraudulent document leads to a criminal case against the Director/Principal and the Chairman/Secretary.
- **2.5:** **EoA is granted on Self-Disclosure**; the Council monitors and takes penal action for non-fulfilment.
- **1.9 (EVC), notes (f) and (g):**
  - an online EVC needs a video of at least 30 minutes on the Trust/Society website, with the link shared on the portal;
  - a physical EVC is video-recorded with date and time;
  - **NOTE:** the video (≥30 min) must cover the committee's entry and exit times, the approved building plan, and **GPS coordinates at the entrance of the main building, academic building, administrative building and library**.
- **Annexure-1 1.2(ix)** (new institutions): about 30 minutes of video covering every room type, uploaded to YouTube with the URL on the portal.

## Annexure-2.1: documents for EoA based on Self-Disclosure
Documents must be authenticated by the Chairman/Secretary, or by the authorised person for government institutions.
1. **Affidavit 2** with the Chairman/Secretary's digital signature, **on e-stamp paper of ₹100**, sworn before a Magistrate, Notary or Oath Commissioner.
2. The pay receipt printed from the AICTE portal (EoA for the year).
3. A valid **Occupancy/Completion Certificate / Building License / Form D** from the competent authority (cl. 7.17).
4. A valid **Structural Stability Certificate**, if the building is more than 30 years past its Completion Certificate.
5. A valid **Fire Safety Certificate**.
6. A **satellite map** showing the land's location with **latitude and longitude at the entrance of the main building**.
7. The **audited statement of accounts** of the Trust/Society/Company for the previous year.

**Other categories:**
- NOC from the affiliating University/Board (Format 2);
- Trust resolution (Format 3);
- Affidavit 8 for an intake increase;
- Affidavit 4 for closure;
- Affidavit 9 for bank changes;
- Affidavit 10 and Registrar approvals for a Trust name change;
- land documents/deeds and proof of working capital (FDR or bank statement plus bank manager certificate) for a Trust name change or merger.

## Affidavit 2: what the college swears to
- **Portal-generated:** "Institute shall download portal generated common affidavit as per application", prints it on ₹100 stamp or e-stamp paper, swears it and uploads it. It declares:
  - the information is true and nothing is concealed;
  - the institution is at its approved location;
  - OC/CC, structural stability and fire certificates are uploaded;
  - the principal is regular and qualified;
  - faculty norms and the faculty:student ratio are met and the faculty data is true;
  - original degree certificates are not retained;
  - land and built-up area are true;
  - the land is **contiguous and undisputed**;
  - salaries follow pay scale with PF/TDS;
  - previous years' student data is true;
  - payments are digital only;
  - the audited accounts are uploaded;
  - the committees (Anti-Ragging, Grievance, IC, SC/ST) exist.
- **Two tables are embedded:**
  - **Land:** document name, deed holder, date of registration, plot no., address, district, area in acres.
  - **Rooms:** room no., room type, carpet area (m²), completion status.

## Chapter VI norms that can be machine-checked
- **6.14:**
  - (a) salaries paid by ECS through nationalised banks; the EVC checks annual salary statements;
  - (c) faculty IDs are added or deleted on the portal **within the same month** someone joins or leaves;
  - (i) **Aadhaar/PAN seeding** for faculty;
  - (j) **biometric attendance** for regular faculty;
  - (f) every PG course needs at least one Professor with a Ph.D.
- **Annexure-5:** faculty:student ratio of 1:20 (UG Engineering/Technology) and 1:15 (PG), with cadre ratio 1:2:6.
- **Annexure-3:**
  - built-up area is calculated from approved intake × course duration;
  - land at most **3 plots, no more than 2 km apart**, with academic, instructional, admin and amenities in one plot;
  - a **partial/provisional OC counts for only 2 consecutive academic years**;
  - the fire certificate is required before applying;
  - structural stability and safe-foundation certificates are needed for added construction.

## Chapter VII penalties
- **7.4(a):** "Non-Submission/ Submission of Incomplete/ False/ Incorrect Information… in Online Application for Extension of Approval" is a violation.
- **7.3 penalties:** reduction in intake, no admission, withdrawal of approval, fines (5× fees per excess admission), and loss of eligibility for AICTE grants.
- **7.1 NOTE:** a false affidavit leads to penal action against the deponent.
- **7.7:** an FDR encashed early carries a 10% penalty.
- **7.10:** complaints go PGRC → SCSC → EVC/SHC → EC (Speaking Order) → appeal.

## Corrections to earlier assumptions
- The **FDR/security deposit** is **not** part of the self-disclosure EoA list (Annexure-2.1). It applies to new institutions (Annexure-1) and Trust changes.
- Land and lease deeds are not separate uploads for self-disclosure EoA. Land details are sworn in the Affidavit 2 table instead.
- **E-stamp:** Annexure-2.1(i) specifies e-stamp for self-disclosure EoA. Elsewhere either "Non-Judicial Stamp Paper/ e-stamp paper" is allowed. Physical stamp paper can't be verified online, so it goes to manual review.
